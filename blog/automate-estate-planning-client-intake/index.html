How to Automate Estate Planning Client Intake

18 August 2026 · Estate Planning

Estate planning client intake is often treated as a form problem. It is really a workflow problem.

What Is Automated Estate Planning Intake?

Client enquiry → intake → information collected → documents requested → missing information identified → matter created → tasks triggered → team notified.

Step 1: Build the Right Intake Form

Collect what the firm actually needs to determine the next step. Avoid asking everything simply because the form allows it.

Step 2: Structure the Information

Structured data is easier to validate, route and reuse. Use free text where client context matters and structured fields where workflow decisions matter.

Step 3: Identify Missing Information

Automatically check required responses and surface gaps early.

Step 4: Request Documents

Turn outstanding items into tracked workflow steps rather than memory-based chasing.

Step 5: Create the Matter

Use approved information to create or prepare the PMS matter and reduce duplicate entry.

Step 6: Trigger the Next Workflow

Matter created → workflow applied → consultation task → team notification → next-step communication.

Step 7: Automate Follow-Ups

Build defined reminders and escalation paths for incomplete matters.

AI vs Traditional Automation

Use deterministic automation for predictable steps. Use AI where free text or unstructured communication needs interpretation.

A Practical Workflow

Form submitted → information checked → missing information identified → documents requested → matter prepared → workflow triggered → lawyer reviews.

Conclusion

The goal is not a better form. It is a better process.
