Why Small Law Firms Should Automate Before They Hire More Admin Staff

20 May 2026 · AI for Law Firms

More work often leads directly to another admin hire. Sometimes that is correct; sometimes it simply scales the manual process.

Some Admin Work Isn’t Really People Work

Repetitive data entry, reminders, task creation and system-to-system movement are automation candidates.

Automation Can Scale Differently

Once a workflow exists, another matter does not necessarily require another full manual setup.

This Doesn’t Mean Don’t Hire

People remain essential for communication, exceptions, coordination and judgment.

Hire People for Judgment, Not Data Entry

Give staff higher-value operational work when routine work can be automated.

Conclusion

Automation should not avoid investing in people; it should avoid wasting their time.
