Why Most AI Tools for Lawyers Don’t Actually Save Lawyers Time

30 April 2026 · Opinion

A tool can look amazing while the user still has to find, copy, review and move information manually.

The Missing Layer

Workflow integration.

Tools Are Still Useful

The point is not to reject AI tools; it is to implement them inside a useful process.

The Most Valuable Automation Is Boring

Routine reminders and data movement can have more operational value than flashy demos.

Ask These Questions

What workflow changes? What manual step disappears? What happens after the AI output? Does it reach the system where work happens?

Conclusion

Do not ask what the AI tool can do. Ask what part of the workflow disappears.
