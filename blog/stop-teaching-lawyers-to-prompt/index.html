Stop Teaching Lawyers to Prompt. Start Automating Their Work.

19 June 2026 · Opinion

Instead of “What should I ask AI?” ask “What work should I stop doing manually?”

Prompting Is Still Manual

Copying context into an AI tool and pasting the output somewhere else is still workflow overhead.

AI Should Disappear Into the Workflow

Lawyers should receive useful outputs rather than constantly operate the AI layer.

Prompting Has a Place

Lawyers should understand AI tools, but prompting is only one layer of implementation.

The Lawyer’s Time Is the Scarce Resource

Expertise and judgment are valuable; repetitive information movement is not.

Conclusion

Stop asking lawyers to do more with AI. Ask what work AI can remove from their day.
