The Biggest AI Mistake Small Law Firms Are Making in 2026

30 May 2026 · Opinion

Using AI without changing the underlying workflow.

Starting With the Tool

Ask what work needs changing before deciding which tool to buy.

Automating a Bad Process

Simplify first, automate second.

Trying to Automate Everything

Use focused workflows and measurable pilots.

Ignoring Existing Software

Connect the systems you already own before buying more.

Giving AI Too Much Authority

Scope permissions and require review for consequential actions.

Not Measuring

Track time, manual steps, errors, response time and follow-up volume.

Building Something Nobody Uses

Design around the people who actually operate the process.

Conclusion

The question is not whether the firm uses AI; it is what work disappeared because of the implementation.
