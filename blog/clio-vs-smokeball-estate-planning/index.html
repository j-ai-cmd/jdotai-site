Clio vs Smokeball for Estate Planning Firms

19 July 2026 · Estate Planning + PMS

Estate planning practices benefit from repeatable workflows, especially around intake and documents.

Start With the Workflow

Enquiry → intake → documents → matter → consultation → drafting → signing → closing.

Clio for Estate Planning

Broad integrations can be attractive when firms want to connect intake, communications, documents and other applications.

Smokeball for Estate Planning

An integrated legal workflow can be attractive for firms that want matters and document workflows closely connected.

Intake Is a Major Factor

Ask what happens after the form, not just whether the product has a form.

Document Management Matters

Evaluate organisation, collection, workflow triggers and staff visibility.

AI Changes the Conversation

Think about the PMS as part of an AI-powered workflow rather than the only place work happens.

Conclusion

Choose the system that makes your actual estate-planning process easiest to operate and automate.
