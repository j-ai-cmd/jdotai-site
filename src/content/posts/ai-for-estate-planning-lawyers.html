AI for Estate Planning Lawyers: 10 Workflows Worth Automating

23 August 2026 · Estate Planning

Estate planning is personal. It is also full of repetitive administrative work. The goal is not to automate estate planning itself; it is to automate the workflow surrounding it.

1. New Client Intake

Connect enquiry → intake → information processing → missing-information checks → matter preparation.

2. Document Collection

Track required documents, request missing items and notify the team when requirements are complete.

3. Matter Creation

Turn approved intake information into contacts, matters, tasks and standard workflows without duplicate entry.

4. Consultation Preparation

Use AI to organise information already collected into an internal preparation summary for lawyer review.

5. Missing Information

Identify obvious gaps in structured or unstructured information and route them for clarification.

6. Client Follow-Up

Use event-driven reminders for incomplete forms, missing documents and scheduled next steps.

7. Internal Task Creation

Standardise what is standard: matter type → relevant task list → owner → next action.

8. Email Triage

Classify incoming messages into document submission, scheduling, administrative request or lawyer-review categories.

9. Post-Meeting Workflows

Meeting completed → notes organised → tasks created → documents requested → follow-up scheduled.

10. Matter Closing

Use workflows to identify incomplete administrative steps before closure.

What Should Stay Human?

Keep legal judgment, advice, strategy and consequential decisions appropriately human-controlled.

Conclusion

The best AI system is not the one that looks impressive in a demo. It is the one that quietly removes unnecessary work from every matter.
