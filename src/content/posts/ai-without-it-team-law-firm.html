How Small Law Firms Can Use AI Without Hiring an IT Team

15 May 2026 · AI for Law Firms

Inventory PMS, email, documents, calendar, forms, accounting and AI tools.

Don’t Build Everything From Scratch

Use existing software and integrations first.

Start With One Workflow

Intake is often a practical starting point.

Use AI Only Where It Adds Value

Deterministic automation for predictable steps; AI for interpretation.

Use the PMS as System of Record

Keep the existing PMS central while adding connected workflow layers.

Keep Humans in the Right Places

AI prepares; the system organises; the lawyer reviews and decides.

Measure One Result

Prove the time or admin reduction of one workflow.

Conclusion

A small firm needs a clear problem and practical implementation more than a technology department.
