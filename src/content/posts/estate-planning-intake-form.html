What Should an Estate Planning Intake Form Ask?

14 July 2026 · Estate Planning + Intake

Before writing questions, ask what the lawyer needs to know before the first meaningful interaction.

Basic Client Information

Collect contact and jurisdiction information relevant to the firm’s process.

Family Information

Capture relevant relationships, spouse/partner, children, beneficiaries and dependants as appropriate.

Existing Estate Planning

Ask whether relevant documents already exist and need review or updating.

Asset Information

Collect the level of asset information the firm actually needs at intake.

Objectives

Give clients a way to explain what they are trying to accomplish in their own words.

Documents

Use the intake process to identify which supporting documents may be relevant, subject to the firm’s process.

Do Not Ask Questions Just Because You Can

If the answer does not change the next step, consider removing the question.

Structure Information for Automation

Use structured fields where workflow decisions matter; keep free text where client context matters.

What Happens After the Form?

Form submitted → information checked → missing items identified → documents requested → matter prepared → workflow triggered → lawyer notified.

Conclusion

The intake form is part of the legal workflow, not a standalone webpage element.
