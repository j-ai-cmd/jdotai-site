AI for Solo Lawyers: What Is Actually Worth Automating?

25 May 2026 · AI for Law Firms

Routine admin, chasing, data entry, repetitive emails, matter setup and follow-up are obvious candidates.

Automate Client Intake

Enquiry → intake → information → documents → matter → notification.

Automate Document Collection

Track outstanding items instead of maintaining a mental list.

Automate Repetitive Emails

Standardise routine messages and personalise only where useful.

Automate Practice Management Setup

Use matter creation to trigger standard workflow tasks.

Make Information Easier to Find

Use defined AI access to retrieve and organise information already in the firm’s systems.

Keep Judgment Human

Professional advice and strategic decisions stay with the lawyer.

Conclusion

The solo-firm advantage is focus: remove admin that does not require you.
