Automate Conflict Checks, Intake and Matter Creation

4 June 2026 · Legal Intake

The workflow begins when the prospective client first contacts the firm.

Automate Intake

Collect prospective client and relevant-party information according to the firm’s established process.

Identify Relevant Names

AI can help extract or structure names from unstructured messages, but final conflict decisions remain with the firm.

Run the Appropriate Check

Route information into the firm’s approved conflict process; do not replace that process with AI.

Matter Creation After Review

Once the matter may proceed, approved data can drive contact/matter creation and workflow setup.

Exceptions

Potential conflict → stop and review; missing information → request clarification; uncertainty → review.

Conclusion

Treat intake, conflict preparation and matter creation as one onboarding workflow.
