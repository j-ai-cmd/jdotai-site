Clio vs Smokeball for Small Law Firms

24 July 2026 · PMS Comparison

Choosing a PMS is choosing where core matter, document, task and workflow data will live.

The Big Difference

Clio is strongly positioned around cloud practice management and a broad integration ecosystem. Smokeball focuses heavily on legal matter management, documents and integrated workflows.

Clio

Potential fit: firms prioritising broad integrations, flexibility and connecting multiple applications.

Smokeball

Potential fit: firms wanting an integrated legal environment centred on matters, documents and workflows.

What About Automation?

Compare each platform against the workflow your firm actually needs, not a generic feature checklist.

What About Integrations?

Ask whether the exact tools in your stack can connect in the exact way your process requires.

What About Estate Planning?

Map intake → documents → matter → tasks → consultation → closing, then compare the products against that process.

What About AI?

The important question increasingly includes how the PMS can participate in AI-powered workflows, not only which features it has natively.

Conclusion

Which system will require the least manual work to run your firm?
