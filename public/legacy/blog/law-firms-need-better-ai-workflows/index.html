Most Law Firms Don’t Need More AI Tools. They Need Better Workflows.

29 June 2026 · Opinion

Buying an AI tool without changing the workflow may simply create another tab.

Start With the Work

Map intake, documents, matter creation, follow-up and information retrieval before selecting software.

Lawyers Shouldn’t Become AI Operators

The best systems reduce the need for lawyers to operate the technology manually.

The Best Automation Is Boring

Reminders, task creation and data movement are not flashy; they are valuable because they repeat.

Where to Look

What happens repeatedly? What gets copied? What gets chased? What gets forgotten? What crosses systems?

AI vs Automation

Use rules for deterministic steps; AI for interpretation.

Small Firms Have an Advantage

They can often change a smaller number of processes without enterprise-level organisational complexity.

Conclusion

The best AI strategy is often one workflow at a time.
