AI Won’t Replace Lawyers — But Lawyers Who Use AI Will Outperform Those Who Don’t.

14 June 2026 · Opinion

AI is unlikely to eliminate the need for lawyers, but it can change how lawyers spend their time.

AI Is Better at Some Things

Repetition, classification, data movement and routine administration are strong automation candidates.

The Real Competitive Advantage Is Time

Two similar firms can differ substantially in how much lawyer time is consumed by admin.

AI Doesn’t Need to Replace the Lawyer

The meaningful comparison is lawyers doing everything manually versus lawyers working with better systems.

The Future Lawyer

The lawyer is still responsible for legal judgment while the system increasingly handles surrounding administrative work.

Conclusion

The competitive advantage is not merely using AI; it is implementing it well.
